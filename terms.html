🛡️ Buyer Protection Policy

🔒 Your Purchases Are Protected

At SPhiri The Artist, we are committed to ensuring every customer has a safe and secure shopping experience. All orders are protected through our Buyer Protection guarantee.

What is Buyer Protection?

Buyer Protection is our commitment to safeguarding your purchases from the moment you place an order until you receive and approve your items. It covers:

  • Verified Orders: All orders are confirmed via email and WhatsApp
  • Secure Payments: All payment methods are processed through secure, encrypted channels
  • Order Tracking: Real-time updates on your order status
  • Dispute Resolution: We work to resolve any issues fairly and promptly

How It Works

When you complete a purchase on SPhiri The Artist:

  1. Order Confirmation: You receive immediate confirmation via email/WhatsApp
  2. Payment Verification: For EFT payments, we verify proof of payment before processing
  3. Order Processing: Your order is prepared with care
  4. Delivery/Collection: You receive tracking info or pickup instructions
  5. Satisfaction: Inspect your order before completing the transaction

For EFT Payments

🏦 EFT Verification Process

When you pay via EFT:

  • Your order is reserved for 48 hours
  • Send proof of payment to contact@sphiri.art or WhatsApp us
  • We verify the payment with our bank
  • Once confirmed, your order is processed
  • If payment isn't received within 48 hours, the order is automatically cancelled

↩️ Refund & Returns Policy

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order under the following conditions:

  • Before Payment: Orders not yet paid can be cancelled anytime
  • After Payment (EFT): Cancellation requested before verification receives full refund
  • Custom Items: Cancellation may incur a 20% handling fee for materials already procured

Refund Eligibility

When Refunds Are Available

  • Item is significantly different from description
  • Item is damaged or defective upon receipt
  • Wrong item was sent
  • Order couldn't be fulfilled due to stock issues

When Refunds May Not Apply

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Custom or personalized items (unless defective)
  • Items damaged after receipt (not due to our handling)
  • Items not in original condition or tags removed
>Refund Process
  1. Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order
  2. Provide your order number and description of the issue
  3. We review your request within 2-3 business days
  4. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method
  5. Refund typically reflects within 5-10 business days
